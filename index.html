FAQ

Questions people ask

These cover what people ask most.

Do purchased credits ever expire?

No. Your credits stay in your wallet permanently until you use them. There are no monthly resets or expiration dates.

What payment methods are supported?

Cashfree securely supports UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), RuPay, Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards, Net Banking across 50+ Indian banks, and leading wallets.

What happens if an AI decision generation fails?

If the AI service hits a network error or cannot complete your analysis, your credits are returned to your balance automatically.

Is this a recurring subscription?

No. DecideForMe AI is 100% pay-as-you-go. You only pay when you choose to buy credits, with no recurring or hidden charges.

What happens to my decisions and voice input?

Your decisions are sent to the AI service to make the analysis and saved to your history. Voice becomes text in your browser, and this site does not store your audio. You can delete a decision, clear history, or delete your account anytime.
